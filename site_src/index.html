Method and licences

Three questions, kept apart

  1. Did the takedown happen? The publisher reports its own action. Source grade A2: reliable on its own enforcement, not confirmable from outside.
  2. Where were the operators? The publisher's judgment, quoted as written, with the publisher's own level (high, moderate, none).
  3. Who directed it? Recorded only where a publisher names an operator. "Aligned with Russia", which the EEAS writes of operations it calls non-attributed, is shown as alignment, never as origin.

Our confidence follows the suite's rubric (tic-confidence-1.0): confidence mapping, method.

What is never kept

Indicator values, account handles, URLs and domains, and the names of individuals. Meta's indicator tables are read only to count their rows. Every sentence kept from a publisher is scrubbed: links become "[link withheld]" and likely personal names "[name withheld]". The rule leans towards withholding.
